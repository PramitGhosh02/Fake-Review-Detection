5. SCIENTIFIC & PRACTICAL TAKEAWAYS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. High-Precision Regime:
   When DeBERTa-v3 flags a review as deceptive, it is correct in 86.74% of cases.
   This drastically reduces wrongful penalization of legitimate sellers/reviewers
   compared to the TF-IDF baseline (which had a ~33% false alarm rate).

2. Trade-Off on Recall:
   At threshold 0.50, recall is 28.09%. Because DeBERTa-v3 is a strictly text-only
   model, subtle promotional text that mimics genuine reviews without obvious
   deceptive syntax cannot be caught on text alone.

3. Motivation for Step 7 (Multimodal Fusion):
   Combining DeBERTa's high-precision text embeddings with tabular behavioral
   metadata (verified_purchase, review frequency, rating deviation) will bridge
   the recall gap without sacrificing precision.
